01 — The human problem

The human problem

Every day, we hold a thousand small details — until we don't.

A quiet kind of labour goes into simply keeping track — of people, of promises, of the thread of a conversation. Most of the time it works. Until the one detail that mattered goes missing.
P·01PeopleWho you know
P·02ConversationsWhat was said
P·03PlacesWhere you've been
P·04AppointmentsWhen, and with whom
P·05PromisesWhat you committed to

Human memory is associative, not exhaustive — it was never designed to hold every detail. YBY exists to carry part of that weight. Quietly, and without judgement.

09 — Offline-first

Offline-first architecture

Intelligence that stays close.

Edge AI means perception, memory and reasoning happen on the device itself — low latency, resilient without connectivity, and private by construction. Try cutting the cord:

Traditional cloud pipelineRound trip · seconds · needs signal
DEVICE INTERNET CLOUD AI RESPONSE
YBYOn device · low latency
DEVICE EDGE AI RESPONSE NO INTERNET REQUIRED
Simulate no connection

The difference is architectural, not incremental.

Local processing

Perception, embeddings and language models run on the glasses — nothing sensitive leaves the device.

Low latency

No round trip means answers arrive in the moment they're useful — not seconds later.

Private by construction

Resilience without connectivity isn't a fallback mode. It's the default architecture.
